## Administrative Boundaries of Armenia and Artsakh

Administrative boundary layers for Armenia and Artsakh in GeoPackage (EPGS:32648) and GeoJSON (WGS84). 

### Datasets

#### `armenia_admin` Republic of Armenia
* Level 1 administrative divisions of the Republic of Armenia. The outer border was digitized from 1:100,000 scale topographic maps of the Soviet General Staff (*Genshtab*). Internal administrative divisions are based on data from the Cadastre Committee of the Republic of Armenia (2023). Northern segment was cross-referenced with data from the National Agency of Public Registry of Georgia.
* **Sources:** Soviet General Staff (*Genshtab*) 1:100,000 scale topographic maps (1975–1990); *Cadastre Committee of the Republic of Armenia*

#### `artsakh_admin_2020_2023` Republic of Artsakh (2020–2023)
* Administrative boundary of the Republic of Artsakh following the 2020 war until September 2023, digitized from the late-2023 situation maps periodically published by the Russian Peacekeeping Forces. The boundary represent the area claimed to be under the control of the peacekeeping forces and does not represent the actual situation or control on the ground at the time.
* **Sources:** [Russian Peacekeeping Forces Situation Map (October 8, 2023 \[sic\])](http://web.archive.org/web/20231013022601/https://mil.ru/files/morf/rarababah-81023-2800.jpg)

#### `artsakh_admin_1994_2020` Republic of Artsakh (1994–2020)
* Level 1 administrative divisions of the Republic of Artsakh (Nagorno-Karabakh Republic) from 1994 to November 2020. Areas designated as "claimed" represent parts of the former Nagorno-Karabakh Autonomous Oblast (NKAO) and the Shahumyan region that were constitutionally claimed by the NKR but remained outside its physical control during this timeframe.
* **Sources:** *Administrative Map of the Nagorno-Karabakh Republic*, 1:200,000 scale, Cadastre Committee of the Republic of Armenia (2005)

#### `NKR_1991_Declaration_NKAO_Shahumyan` Nagorno-Karabakh Republic: NKAO and Shahumyan Region (1991)

* The territorial scope of the Nagorno-Karabakh Republic (NKR) based on its Declaration of Independence adopted im 1991. The dataset encompasses the boundaries of the former Nagorno-Karabakh Autonomous Oblast (NKAO) and the Shahumyan region.
* **Sources:** Soviet General Staff (*Genshtab*) 1:100,000 scale topographic maps (1975–1990); *Administrative Map of the Nagorno-Karabakh Republic*, 1:200,000 scale, Cadastre Committee of the Republic of Armenia (2005)

### Important Notes

The data is provided "as-is". While care was taken to ensure objective representation, the dataset should still be considered opinionated.
